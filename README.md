# Better or Worse?

Since about 1980, has life in the U.S. gotten better or worse? Guess on 29 indicators (health, safety, money and housing, family and school, environment), then see the real trends, your score, and whether your misses leaned pessimistic or optimistic.

**Live site:** https://gsharpokie.github.io/better-or-worse/

## Data

All figures come from U.S. federal statistical agencies and other primary sources (NCHS/CDC, Census Bureau, FBI, BJS, NHTSA, EPA, EIA, Harvard JCHS). Each card lists its sources and notes method changes that affect comparisons. "About the same" means a change of less than 5% between the first and last year shown. Data compiled October 31, 2026.
