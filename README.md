# Better or Worse?

Since about 1980, has life in the U.S. gotten better or worse? Guess on 29 indicators (health, safety, money and housing, family and school, environment), then see the real trends, your score, and whether your misses leaned pessimistic or optimistic.

**Live site:** https://gsharpokie.github.io/better-or-worse/

## Data

All figures come from U.S. federal statistical agencies and other primary sources (NCHS/CDC, Census Bureau, FBI, BJS, NHTSA, EPA, EIA, Harvard JCHS). Each card lists its sources and notes method changes that affect comparisons. "About the same" means a change of less than 5% between the first and last year shown. Data compiled October 31, 2026.

## Accessibility

This site aims to meet [WCAG 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) Level AA, so it works for people who use screen readers, keyboards, or screen magnification.

- All buttons, sliders, and links can be reached and used with a keyboard, and show a visible outline when selected.
- Charts have text descriptions, and many also have a data table or a slider that reads out each value.
- Text meets WCAG AA color contrast standards.
- Pages fit narrow phone screens without sideways scrolling.

I checked it in November 2026 with automated accessibility testing (axe-core) plus keyboard and small-screen checks. It hasn’t yet been tested by people who use screen readers every day, so there may be problems I haven’t caught. If something doesn’t work for you, please [let me know](https://github.com/gsharpokie/better-or-worse/issues/new).
